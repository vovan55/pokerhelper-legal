Privacy Policy

Last updated: [06.11.2026]

This Privacy Policy describes how PokerHelperPRO ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little data as possible. The App may collect the following types of information:

  • Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics): If the App crashes, it sends anonymous crash reports (device model, OS version, stack trace) to Google Firebase to help us fix bugs.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Google AdMob): When using the free version, the App displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob collects your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to serve personalized ads.
  • Purchase History (Google Play Billing): If you purchase a subscription, Google Play processes the transaction. We do not store your payment details.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely for:

  • Improving the App and fixing errors.
  • Displaying ads (in the free version) to support development.
  • Providing purchase features (if you choose to remove ads).

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. However, we use third-party SDKs that may collect data according to their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (for ads): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (for crash reporting): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (for payments).

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Google Settings -> Ads).
  • Delete the App, which will remove all local data associated with it.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
